Reading this as a business case

Today

Classical solvers win on small and single-period problems. Quantum methods in this lab find valid, good plans, but simulated annealing and an exact solver are faster and better at this size.

The gap

Multi-period planning with even flow is hard. The exact solver could not prove the optimum for 25 stands within 30 s. Real districts have thousands of stands, which is why planners split problems and accept approximations.

The bet

A small pilot buys readiness, not savings. Formulations, benchmarks and skills built now pay off first through quantum-inspired solvers, and later on quantum hardware if it scales. 1% better planning on LVM revenue is worth about €5.9 m a year.
